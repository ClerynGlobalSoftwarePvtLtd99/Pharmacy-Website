SECTION 1
Project Overview & Objectives
What we are building, why, and how it benchmarks against the industry leader.
1.1 Project Background
Layte LifeScience Pvt. Ltd. is a pharmaceutical products dealer and supplier based in New Delhi, India. The
company supplies a wide range of products — including injectable antibiotics, antifungal creams, syrups, tonics,
eye & ear drops, surgical goods, vaccines, and diagnostic products — to semi-regulated and non-regulated
markets across India and internationally.
The current website (laytelifesciencepvtltd.vercel.app) is a single-page scroll site that lacks the depth, credibility,
and visual authority expected by institutional buyers, hospitals, pharmacy chains, and B2B partners in the
pharmaceutical sector. The client requires a complete redesign that delivers the same trust, professionalism,
and brand authority as Dr. Reddy's Laboratories (drreddys.com), one of India's most respected pharmaceutical
companies.
1.2 Business Goals
Primary Goal
Build a 7–10 page professional pharmaceutical website that instills
confidence, credibility, and trust among institutional buyers, hospital
procurement managers, pharmacy chains, and B2B partners.
Design Reference
Match the visual feel, design language, and trust architecture of drreddys.com
— the gold standard for Indian pharma web presence.
Brand Positioning
Elevate Layte LifeScience from 'local pharma dealer' to 'quality-driven national
pharmaceutical supplier' through design, content, and information
architecture.
Conversion Goal
Drive enquiries, partnership requests, and product quote submissions through
clear CTAs and B2B-focused content.
SEO Foundation
Multi-page architecture with proper meta, headings, and structured content for
organic discovery.
1.3 Scope of Work
 Complete redesign of homepage with cinematic hero, stats counter, and trust elements
 Creation of 7 primary pages (Homepage, About, Products, Quality, Capabilities, Leadership, Contact)
 Implementation of Dr. Reddy's-inspired color scheme and design language
 Mega-menu navigation architecture replacing flat anchor links
 Video integration strategy (3 key placements)
 Certifications & compliance section with badge display
 Mobile-responsive layout across all pages
 WhatsApp enquiry integration and multi-department contact form
 Stats counter section with animated numbers on scroll

4.2 Video Production Notes
 If professional video is not immediately available, use a high-quality stock video of pharmaceutical
manufacturing/laboratory as a TEMPORARY hero placeholder — but replace with real footage ASAP.
 Hero video should be hosted on your own server or CDN (not YouTube) — it should autoplay, loop, be
muted, and load fast. Recommended format: MP4, H.264, 1920x1080, under 8MB.
 Director message videos (V2, V3) can be filmed with a good smartphone in a clean, well-lit room —
professional quality editing is more important than expensive equipment.
 Add subtitles to all spoken-word videos — many users browse with sound off on mobile.
 On mobile devices, show a static image fallback instead of autoplay video for performance.
 Thumbnail for embedded videos: use a professional still frame with a centered play button overlay.
 All videos must have proper lighting, clear audio (if spoken), and a clean, uncluttered background.
