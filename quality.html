Our Commitment to Quality

Beyond compliance — quality is embedded in every product we supply, every process we follow, and every partnership we build.

Quality Is Not a Department — It Is Our Culture

At Layte LifeScience, quality is not an afterthought — it is the foundation. We source exclusively from manufacturers who meet the highest national and international regulatory standards, and we conduct our own verification before any product enters our supply chain.

We believe that every medicine we distribute has a patient at the end. That responsibility drives us to maintain zero tolerance for substandard products.

Regulatory Approvals & Certifications

Our products and suppliers are approved and registered under the following regulatory bodies:

DCGI

Drug Controller General of India — central authority for pharmaceutical approvals.

CDSCO

Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation oversight for drug quality and safety.

WHO-GMP

World Health Organisation Good Manufacturing Practice aligned manufacturing partners.

ISO 9001:2015

Quality Management System frameworks for consistent, auditable processes.

Drugs & Cosmetics Act

Strict legal compliance and operational audits under the Indian Drugs and Cosmetics Act and international FDA regulations, as authorized by our MoA.

Delhi Drug Licence

Wholesale distribution and chemical trading license issued by the Drugs Control Department, Government of NCT of Delhi.

GST Registered Business

Fully compliant GST-registered entity with proper invoice documentation.
