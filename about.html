About Layte LifeScience

A decade of trust. A commitment to quality. A partner you can count on.

Our Mission

To ensure that quality medicines reach every hand — from metro hospitals to rural clinics — with integrity, reliability, and compassion.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted pharmaceutical supply partner in India, known for unwavering quality, transparent practices, and patient-first values.

Our Journey

Layte LifeScience Private Limited was incorporated in Delhi in March 2026 as a premier private limited entity designed to formalize and scale the co-founders' over a decade-long path in pharmaceutical trading, B2B procurement, and distribution logistics.

By integrating this extensive founding experience under a unified corporate structure, we quickly grew our portfolio to over 500 high-quality pharmaceutical formulations and active substances (APIs), serving clinical networks and pharmacies across 20+ Indian states.

Our registered office and central operations are strategically located in New Delhi—the heart of India's healthcare supply chain—enabling swift and temperature-controlled dispatch to hospitals and regional distribution centers.
