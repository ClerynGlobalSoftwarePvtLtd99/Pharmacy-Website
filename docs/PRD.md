# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Overview
**Product Name:** Layte LifeScience Private Limited Corporate Website  
**Domain:** Pharmaceuticals & Healthcare  
**Business Profile:** Wholesale trading, distribution, manufacturing, importing/exporting, and marketing of pharmaceutical and medical goods (medicines, APIs, surgical goods, medical devices, diagnostic products, etc.).  

## Objectives
- Create a premium-themed, responsive corporate website that establishes Layte LifeScience's online presence.
- Showcase the comprehensive product portfolio described in the company's Memorandum of Association (MOA), including medicines, APIs, surgical devices, to attract potential global and domestic clients.
- Provide clear avenues for B2B inquiries, partnerships, and franchisee opportunities.

## Target Audience
- Healthcare providers, pharmacies, and clinics looking for reliable wholesale suppliers.
- B2B partners for franchise opportunities or distribution networks.
- Pharmaceutical researchers and API buyers.

## Key Features & Requirements
### 1. Branding & Identity
- **Name:** Layte LifeScience Private Limited
- **Location:** Delhi, India
- **Aesthetic:** Clean, modern, trustworthy ("premium" medical theme). Primary colors: Teal (`#11b3c3`), Yellow (`#e2cc15`), and professional grays/whites. Micro-animations and rich glassmorphism elements to engage users.

### 2. Core Pages / Sections
- **Hero Section:** High-impact visual emphasizing quality-driven pharmaceuticals. Separate Mobile/Desktop backgrounds. Clear CTA ("Enquire Now").
- **Categories:** Must highlight core business lines from the MOA:
  - Injectables / Antibiotics
  - Antifungal / Antibacterial
  - Syrups & Tonics
  - *New:* Surgical Goods & Medical Devices (As per MOA object #1)
  - *New:* Vaccines & Serums (As per MOA object #1, #2)
- **Products Listing (Best Sellers):** Product cards featuring image, price, description, and "Buy/Enquire" functionality.
- **About Us:** Summarizes the MOA's objectives: functioning in semi-regulated/non-regulated global markets, commitment to quality, research, and API manufacturing.
- **Testimonials & Gallery:** Social proof and facility/product images.
- **Contact Us:** Address (Delhi, India), direct email (`laytelifescience@gmail.com`), phone number, business hours, interactive Google Map embedded for Delhi, and an inquiry form.

### 3. Technical Requirements
- **Frontend Stack:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS v3 (via CDN), Vanilla JS.
- **Responsiveness:** Mobile-first approach using responsive utilities.
- **Performance:** Optimized images, lazy-loaded components, semantic structure.
- **SEO & Tracking:** Meta descriptions, relevant keywords, semantic headers (H1, H2). 

### 4. Future Expansion (Phase 2)
- **E-commerce capabilities** for direct product ordering.
- **Franchisee Portal** to cater specifically to MOA object #6. 
- **Dynamic Content Management (CMS):** For updating products, categories, and blogs.

## Open Questions & Dependencies
- Review finalized list of categories/products for launch.
- Availability of specific high-quality product images for Surgical Goods, Vaccines, etc.
- Confirmed business email and phone number for live deployment.
